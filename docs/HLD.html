1. Executive Summary & Product Mission

SocialForge is an enterprise-grade, multi-tenant AI social media automation and orchestration platform engineered for marketing agencies, multi-brand holding companies, and digital creators. The platform consolidates the entire marketing lifecycle into a closed-loop operating system:

Core Value Architecture

  • Multi-Brand Tenant Isolation: Strict data segregation across tenants, brands, media assets, social accounts, and billing quotas.
  • 16-Dimension Brand Brain: Autonomous extraction of brand voice, target personas, tone, style rules, taboo words, and mandatory disclaimers from public URLs or manual entry.
  • AI Campaign Arc Generator: 1-click transformation of business concepts into multi-day narrative arcs across Instagram, Facebook, X, LinkedIn, and TikTok.
  • Multimodal Creative Studio: Native copy composition, Canva-like visual poster generation, and native FFmpeg 9:16 vertical short-form video synthesis with synchronized subtitles and audio ducking.
  • Magic Client Approval Workflow: Zero-friction client review portals accessible via cryptographically signed tokens—no client login required.
  • Resilient Hybrid Publishing Engine: Production OAuth 2.0 adapters for Meta, X, LinkedIn, and TikTok, alongside an instant Mock Sandbox Simulator.
  • Closed-Loop SaaS Monetization: Full Stripe subscription billing, granular usage quotas, 3-tier dunning recovery, and an automated superadmin revenue telemetry dashboard.

4. Functional Specification & Current Features

Feature Spotlight: 3-Tab Campaigns Page

The campaign engine orchestrates complex multi-channel marketing campaigns through an intuitive three-tab interface:

  • Tab 1: CAMPAIGN GENERATOR — Multi-step AI wizard transforming raw ideas into 12 Strategic Dimensions and multi-post content sequences.
  • Tab 2: Active Brand Campaigns — Visual portfolio displaying all saved campaigns, post counts, progress bars, and execution states.
  • Tab 3: Selected Sprint Arc Drilldown — Granular multi-post timeline with platform-native variant editing, single-post AI rewrite actions, 1-click batch scheduling, and 1-click batch publishing.

Feature Spotlight: 16-Dimension Brand Brain

Serves as the deterministic voice profile for each managed brand:

Includes automatic URL scraping, target persona definitions, tone formality matrices, writing rhythm rules, signature emoji density, taboo buzzwords, and mandatory legal disclaimers.

Feature Spotlight: Multimodal Creative Studios

  • Content Studio: 10 Content Formats + 11 AI Style Transformations with live device previews for Instagram, X, LinkedIn, TikTok, and Facebook.
  • Canva Visual Editor: Layered graphic composition across 4 aspect ratios (1:1, 4:5, 1.91:1, 9:16) with SVG rasterization.
  • Reel Motion Studio: Native FFmpeg 9:16 short-form video generation with 4-beat narrative arcs, hardcoded ASS subtitles, and dynamic audio ducking.

7. Security, Compliance & Isolation Architecture

Security Vector Threat Vector Mitigated Technical Implementation
Multi-Tenancy Isolation Cross-tenant data exposure Enforced tenant_id query scoping on all Knex SQL statements and database foreign key cascades.
AES-256-GCM Vault Credential theft at rest Unique 12-byte initialization vectors (IV) and 16-byte authentication tags for all stored social tokens.
SSRF Defense Shield Cloud metadata leakage & internal port probing Hostname resolution blocking loopback (127.0.0.1), RFC1918 subnets, and AWS/GCP metadata IP (169.254.169.254).
Sliding-Window Limiter Brute-force auth attacks & DDoS In-memory sliding window rate limiter returning standard HTTP 429 and Retry-After headers.
Publishing Idempotency Duplicate post dispatches on network retries Transactional status locking to publishing prior to adapter invocation.
