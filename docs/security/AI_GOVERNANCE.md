# AI Governance & Safety Policies

## Overview
Defines policy enforcement, tone compliance, and human oversight for autonomous AI agents.

## Safety Controls
- **Human-in-the-Loop Gates**: Posts exceeding predefined confidence, tone risk, or financial promotion limits require explicit human review.
- **Negative Prompting & Restrictions**: Workspaces configure banned terms, restricted topics, and competitor references that the AI must never generate.
- **Audit Logging**: Every AI prompt, raw response, token consumption, and model parameter is logged for full provenance.
