# SaaS Pricing & Subscription Tiers

## Subscription Plans
1. **Starter ($29/mo)**:
   - 3 Social Accounts, 1 Workspace, 100 AI Posts/month, Standard Templates.
2. **Professional ($79/mo)**:
   - 10 Social Accounts, 3 Workspaces, 500 AI Posts/month, Connect Hub integrations, Video Reel Engine.
3. **Agency ($199/mo)**:
   - Unlimited Social Accounts, Unlimited Workspaces, 2,500 AI Posts/month, External Client Magic Links, Custom Brand Kits, Dedicated Priority Support.
4. **Enterprise (Custom)**:
   - Custom SLAs, Dedicated DB instances, Custom Connectors, Single Sign-On (SSO).
