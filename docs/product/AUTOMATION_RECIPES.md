# Automation Recipes

## Pre-Built Automation Recipes
1. **Google Sheets to Multi-Platform Publisher**:
   - Trigger: New row added in Google Sheets.
   - Action: Format caption per platform, generate hashtags, schedule post.
2. **Shopify Product Drop Campaign**:
   - Trigger: New product published in Shopify.
   - Action: AI writes 3 variations of launch post, generates 9:16 video reel, queues for team approval.
3. **Weekly High-Performing Content Recycler**:
   - Trigger: Weekly cron schedule.
   - Action: Identify top 10% posts by engagement over last 90 days, rewrite hooks, and reschedule.
4. **Google Drive Asset Watcher**:
   - Trigger: New video uploaded to "Final Renders" folder in Google Drive.
   - Action: Transcribe video, extract hooks, generate caption, draft Instagram Reel.
