HoaspForge — Complete Application Features Specification

Architectural guide, technical blueprints, and operational manual covering all 11 core system domains, commercial SaaS capabilities, and containerized deployment infrastructure.

1. Executive Platform Overview & Architectural Topology

HoaspForge is an enterprise-grade, multi-tenant autonomous social marketing platform designed for agencies, e-commerce brands, and marketing teams. The system continuously ingests business catalog data, synthesizes platform-native copy and visual creatives, enforces strict brand voice and compliance guardrails, routes content through zero-login client approval portals, and automatically schedules and dispatches posts to social networks with real-time performance telemetry.

2. Multi-Tenant Workspaces & Identity Governance

HoaspForge enforces strict row-level multi-tenant data isolation and role-based access control.

🏢 Workspace Scoping

Every resource (brands, posts, campaigns, automations, templates, files) is partitioned by tenant_id. Cross-tenant queries are blocked at the ORM/database layer with multi-workspace switching for agency power users.

🛡️ Role-Based Access (RBAC)

Owner: Full workspace & billing authority.
Admin: Team invitations, brand kit creation & automation.
Creator: Post drafting, studio generation & asset uploads.

🔐 Enterprise Encryption

AES-256-GCM envelope encryption for all third-party access tokens and API secrets. Argon2id password hashing and strict HTTP security headers.

3. Brand Brain & Multi-Brand Voice System (16-in-1 Brand Kit)

Each workspace can manage multiple distinct brand identities via the Brand Brain engine (/brands):

  • Brand Identity & Typography: Primary font family, heading font, body font, and mono font tokens.
  • Dynamic Color Palette: Primary brand color, secondary color, accent tone, canvas background, and surface elevations.
  • Logo & Watermark Engine: Dark mode logo, light mode logo, and automated canvas watermark placement.
  • Tone & Voice Guidelines: Configurable tone profiles (e.g., Witty & Energetic, Authoritative & Clinical, Minimalist Luxury).
  • Brand Vocabulary & Keywords: Mandated signature keywords and approved brand taglines.
  • Taboo & Blocked Words: Real-time linting engine that flags forbidden phrases or non-compliant industry terms.
  • Target Audience Demographics: Persona definitions feeding context into the AI copywriting synthesis prompt.
  • Brand Handle & Social Slugs: Automated hashtag and handle attribution (e.g., @brand_handle).

4. Channel Connect Hub & Business Data Ingestion (Stages 1 – 3)

The Connect Hub (/channels & /connections) connects HoaspForge to social media platforms and upstream business data catalogs:

  • Meta Graph API v20.0: OAuth 2.0 handshake for Facebook Pages and Instagram Professional (Creator & Business). Includes a Mock Sandbox Environment for end-to-end dry-run testing.
  • Google Sheets Ingestion: Continuous row-by-row sync from live Google Sheets spreadsheets.
  • CSV / Excel Ingestion: Direct bulk upload of product catalogs with automated column mapping.
  • Shopify Product Webhooks: Real-time ingestion of newly created or updated e-commerce items.
  • Marketing Object Normalization: Normalizes external data into immutable marketing_objects records with SHA-256 idempotency deduplication.

5. Creative Suite: Content Studio, Canva Studio & Video Engine

✍️ Content Studio

Synthesizes platform-native copy variants for Instagram (hooks + hashtags), Facebook (conversational + CTA), LinkedIn, X, and TikTok. Incorporates a Zero Token Leak Guard preventing credential leaks.

🎨 AI Design Studio (Canva)

Vector visual canvas supporting presets: 1:1 Square, 4:5 Portrait, 9:16 Stories/Reels, 16:9 Landscape, and 2:3 Pinterest. Includes AI Poster Generator across 5 creative styles.

🎬 Reel Motion Studio

Short-form 9:16 video composition engine with kinetic typography, video b-roll overlays, and server-side FFmpeg rendering.

6. Stage 4 Versioned Template Engine & Catalog

The Template Catalog (/templates and /templates/editor/:id) provides enterprise graphic layout governance:

  • 30 Built-in Canonical Templates across 7 categories (Product, Offer, Education, Trust, Company, Event, Vertical) and 22 subcategories.
  • WCAG 2.1 AA Contrast Scoring: Mathematical luminance ratio scoring (requires >= 4.5:1 for body, >= 3.0:1 for bold headers) guaranteeing visual accessibility.
  • Dynamic Variable Binding: Automatic variable replacement (e.g., {{product_name}}, {{price}}, {{discount}}).
  • Immutable Version Branching: Active templates freeze on edit/publication into template_versions, automatically branching to a new active version with instant rollback support.
  • AI Mutation Protection: AI agents are permitted to search, select, and populate template parameters, but are strictly blocked (AI_MUTATION_RESTRICTED) from deleting or altering template definitions.

7. Autonomous AI Growth Agent & Workflow Automations

The autonomous execution layer provides trigger-action automation pipelines (/automations) and agent governance (/ai):

  • Autonomous Fleet: Configurable autonomy levels from L1 Assistive (Drafts only) to L4 Autonomous.
  • Trigger-Action DAGs: Triggers (Cron schedules, Google Sheet row added, Webhook ingress) chained to actions (AI copy, template rendering, approval link, publishing).
  • Prebuilt Recipes: Flagship Product Promotion Arc, Flash Sale Countdown Engine, Weekly Educational Series, and Review Spotlight.
  • Dead-Letter Queue (DLQ): Fail-safe queue handling failed runs with full stack trace inspection, replay, and discard controls.

8. Governance, Compliance & Human Approval Gate (Stage 8)

HoaspForge guarantees that no autonomous process can publish unvetted material to public social feeds:

flowchart LR A["Draft Post Created"] -->|Enforce Zero Silent Publishing| B["In Review Status"] B -->|Generate 64-char Hex Token| C["72-Hour Magic Review Portal"] C -->|Client Mobile Sign-off| D["Approved Status"] D -->|Queue Worker Dispatch| E["Published with Remote IDs"]
  • Zero Silent Publishing Mandate: All posts are initialized strictly in draft status.
  • 72-Hour Zero-Login Magic Review Links: Mobile-first review portal (/review/:token) allowing clients to approve or request edits without a platform account.
  • Full Audit Trail: Records reviewer email, IP, and timestamp.

9. Closed-Loop Performance Analytics & Growth Intelligence

The Growth Intelligence suite (/analytics) closes the loop between publication and future content creation:

  • Real Telemetry Ingestion: Ingests Reach, Impressions, Clicks, Engagement Rate, Comments, and Shares directly from Meta Graph API.
  • Algorithmic Winner Detection: Identifies high-velocity posts and feeds attributes back into the Brand Brain prompt context.
  • Hourly & Daily Rollups: Timeseries analytics stored in post_analytics_metrics.

Autonomy Engine Levels (L1 to L5) Explained

  • L1 (Manual Mode): Posts, copy, and visual templates are created manually by team members; scheduling requires explicit human date-time entry.
  • L2 (Assisted AI — Starter Tier): AI provides copywriting prompts, hashtags, and template adaptations on demand; human must trigger and approve every dispatch.
  • L3 (Supervised Autonomy — Growth Tier): The autonomous agent drafts posts based on campaign schedules and product catalogs. Automatically routes drafts to approval queues; dispatches once approved.
  • L4 (High Autonomy — Agency Pro Tier): The agent creates campaigns, runs A/B variant copy generation, and dispatches routine posts automatically under predefined risk scores; alerts humans only on brand voice anomalies.
  • L5 (Full Autonomy — Scale & Enterprise Tiers): Continuous self-optimizing loop. The AI agent analyzes audience retention metrics, identifies top-performing visual formats, drafts and dispatches content, and recalibrates posting intervals automatically.

Multi-Dimensional Quota Metering & Warning Alerts

Tenant consumption is tracked in real-time across 7 dimensions (Posts, Social Channels, Brands, Seats, AI Tokens, Video Render Seconds, and S3 Storage Bytes).

  • 75% Usage Threshold: Non-intrusive in-app banner notifying workspace admin.
  • 90% Usage Threshold: High-priority dashboard banner and email advisory.
  • 100% Usage Threshold: Graceful degradation (drafting remains enabled; automated publish pauses until billing cycle reset or instant 1-click tier upgrade).
  • Zero Data Deletion Guarantee: Past posts, analytics, media assets, and connected channel credentials are never deleted when limits are reached or during billing hiatus.
  • Stripe Customer Portal: Self-service upgrade, downgrade, payment method updates, and cancel-at-period-end with automated 7-day dunning grace periods.
